How to know the user ordered and paid

  1. Customer adds products to cart and submits checkout.
  2. Your backend creates an order with status Pending Payment.
  3. Payment gateway creates a unique QR for that order ID and exact amount.
  4. Customer pays by QR.
  5. Gateway sends a webhook to your website.
  6. Your order status changes to Paid automatically.

Important: screenshots are not reliable proof. A real paid-order system needs a backend, database, payment gateway, webhook, and admin dashboard.
